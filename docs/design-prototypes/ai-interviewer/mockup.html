Sofie's AI Playbook

Your guide to working with AI in Marketing · built for you

Where you are today

You're already using Copilot most weeks, which puts you ahead of where a lot of marketing teams are — you're comfortable asking AI for help, you've just not yet pointed it at the work that eats your week. The product-description grind between your PIM and WordPress is the obvious place to start: it's repetitive, it's yours end-to-end, and it's exactly the kind of task AI is good at. Let's turn the tool you already trust onto the work that actually costs you time.

Your AI use-cases

1
First-draft product descriptions
Kills the two-day copy-rewrite grind each launch
★ START HERE Quick Win ▾

In practice: Paste the raw PIM spec into Copilot with a short instruction ("turn this into a 60-word product description in our tone"). It drafts; you edit and paste into WordPress. No new tool, nothing to install.

BeforeRewriting dozens of descriptions by hand — ~2 days a launch.
AfterEditing solid drafts — most of an afternoon.
Assumption to check: that PIM specs can be copied out as text (you mentioned copy-pasting, so likely yes).
2
Weekly marketing report summary
Turns your Excel exports into a written summary
Worth a Session ▾

In practice: Drop the weekly Excel export into Copilot and ask for the three things that changed and why — a first draft of the commentary you write by hand.

BeforeEyeballing the sheet, writing commentary from scratch.
AfterEditing an AI first-pass in minutes.
3
Outlook reply drafting
Clears routine email faster
Quick Win ▾

In practice: Use Copilot in Outlook to draft replies to routine requests, then tweak and send.

4
Shared campaign-brief starter Worth a Session
Cross-functional — with Sales
↔ Cross-team ▾

In practice: A shared prompt that turns a product launch into both a campaign brief (for you) and quote-ready copy (for Sales), so Sales stops re-tweaking your tone.

5
PIM → WordPress sync assistant
The destination, not the starting point
Bigger Build ▾

In practice: A proper integration that pushes AI-drafted descriptions from the PIM into WordPress automatically. Worth doing once use-case #1 proves the value — needs IT involvement, so it's the goal to build toward, not where you start.

Your next moves

1
Start with your product descriptions (#1).
Your biggest time-sink, and you can begin today in Copilot — the fastest way to feel the value.
2
Then add the weekly report summary (#2).
Once drafting feels natural, point the same habit at your Friday reporting.
3
Bring in Sales on the campaign-brief starter (#4).
Turn your first wins into something a colleague benefits from too.

What might be holding you back

💬 "I don't have time to learn another tool." ▾
The real concern: your week is already full; a new tool feels like more work before it's less.
Reframe: #1 uses Copilot — the tool you already use. Nothing new to learn, just pointed at a slower task.
First proof: draft five descriptions this week and compare to doing them by hand.
💬 "I don't fully trust what it writes." ▾
The real concern: your name is on the copy; a wrong claim is your problem.
Reframe: you stay the editor — AI does the first draft, you keep final say. It removes the blank page, not your judgement.
First proof: take one AI draft and mark every change you make — you'll see how close it gets.

Do this today (15 minutes)

Open Copilot, paste the spec of one product you're launching soon, and ask: "Write a 60-word product description in a friendly, professional tone." Edit it once. That's use-case #1, started.
